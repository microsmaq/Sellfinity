Sellfinity Tracking Helper

On Fulfillment, use Refresh Amazon & eBay for tracking or Check Amazon prices before purchasing. The helper reads the corresponding signed-in Amazon pages and returns the results to Sellfinity.

  1. Stay signed in to Amazon.
  2. Keep the Fulfillment page open while a check runs.
  3. Review the updated tracking, price, shipping, and profit.

The helper only opens Amazon links supplied by Sellfinity and returns tracking or price details to the matching fulfillment order.
